HOW TO WIN

Deathmatch. First to the score limit takes the match; if the clock runs out, the highest score wins.

THE JETPACK IS THE GAME

Fuel drains while you thrust and refills the moment you stop. Fly in bursts, use walls to slide, and remember that every shot kicks you backwards — recoil is a movement tool.

LOADOUT

Your sidearm is tied to your hero and never runs out of reserve ammo. Primary weapons come from crates and are dropped when dry.
